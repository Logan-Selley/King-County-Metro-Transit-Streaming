King County Metro, Live

A week of King County Metro's vehicle positions and arrival predictions, polled every 20 seconds and streamed through Redpanda and Flink into PostGIS. Where buses bunch, how wrong the arrival predictions are, and whether a replay of the archived bytes reproduces what the live pipeline wrote.

When buses bunch

Alerts by hour, as a rate per day, Pacific time. Two buses on the same route and direction within 1,000 ft, in two consecutive one-minute windows, after the terminal gate. Weekdays and weekends are each divided by their own number of days, so the more numerous weekdays do not outweigh the weekend.

weekday weekend

Which routes

Raw counts favour frequent routes: bunching needs a headway short enough to collapse. The PM peak share is the share of a route's alerts between 16:00 and 18:59.

How wrong the arrival predictions are

Trip-update predictions joined to the arrivals they predicted, grouped by how far ahead each was issued. The typical miss grows with lead time, and the mean error is positive at every forward lead: the estimates run pessimistic, so buses arrive earlier than the sign promised.
